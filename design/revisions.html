How revisions work

  • Editing the published plan copies it into a new draft.
  • One person edits a draft at a time. If their lease expires, another Facilities Admin can take over; this is logged.
  • Publishing archives the current revision and makes the draft live in one step.
  • Assignments follow each desk into the new revision. Removing an assigned desk blocks publishing.
  • Archived revisions stay viewable and can’t be changed.
