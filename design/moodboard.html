Design direction

Drawn like a floor plan

DSKT’s users draw real floors and hand out real desks. The look borrows from architectural drawings: ink linework on a quiet grid, hatched structure, dashed zones and measurements in metres. The interaction model borrows from canvas editors. Workplace views borrow the search-first habits of desk-booking tools, without their live-presence and booking features, which are outside the MVP.

References

What each one taught us, and what we deliberately leave behind. These are product patterns, not assets: nothing is copied.

Arcada Planner

MIT, React + Konva
Take
Catalog on the left, drag a shape onto the plan, snapped preview before drop. Konva layering. Zustand-sized editor store.
Leave
Its local JSON document, free-angle walls, manual hit-testing and print flow. DSKT uses orthogonal polygons and backend commands.

Upstream mehanix/arcada is Apache-2.0, not MIT. Only reviewed MIT portions of the fork may be reused, with notices kept (AGENTS.md, engineering rule 5).

github.com/fedepaj/arcada-planner

Figma and Excalidraw

Canvas conventions
Take
V / H / hold-Space tools, wheel zoom around the cursor, a visible Zoom %, an inspector that always describes the selection, marquee select.
Leave
Resize handles and free rotation. Placements only move and quarter-turn, so DSKT shows corner markers, not draggable resize handles.
excalidraw.com

Skedda

Interactive 2D floor plans
Take
The floor plan is the main way into the data. Click a desk, see who and when.
Leave
Availability shown by fill colour alone, and the booking framing. DSKT records long-running Assignments, so status uses glyph shape plus text.
skedda.com

Robin and deskbird

Find a colleague
Take
Search first, then show the person’s desk on the map with a clear locate ring. A non-canvas list for every result.
Leave
Live presence, occupancy heatmaps and calendar sync. Attendance is deferred in DSKT.
robinpowered.com, deskbird.com

OfficeSpace

Move management
Take
Planner-grade batch work: select many spaces, preview every change, apply all or none.
Leave
Scenario planning and AI layout suggestions. Both are deferred.

Architectural drawings

Line weights, hatching, title blocks
Take
Heavy boundary line, hatched structure for Obstacles, dashed Zones, dimensions in metres, a sheet caption naming site, floor and revision.
Leave
CAD density. Plans stay readable for planners who aren’t architects.

Palette

Light theme is drafting film: grey-green paper, graphite ink, and a grid in non-photo blue, the pencil colour drafters use for construction lines. Buttons are drawn in ink. Selection is a highlighter stroke. Dark theme is a cyanotype blueprint. Category colours come from a coloured-pencil set, and every status pairs its colour with a shape and a word.

Principles

Two workflows, two faces

Facilities talks in templates, vertices and grid. Workplace talks in people and spaces. The Workplace map has no geometry tools at all.

Scale is not Zoom

Editing Scale sits next to Zoom with its own label and units, so nobody confuses snapping with magnification.

Preview, then all or nothing

Imports, batch labels and publishing show every effect first and apply together. Blocked actions say why and how to fix them.

The canvas is never the only way

Every map selection has an HTML panel and every space is reachable through search.
